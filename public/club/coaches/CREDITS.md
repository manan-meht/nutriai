# Placeholder coach photos

Stand-in imagery so the marketplace shows what it looks like populated. Used
only where a coach has no photo of their own — the moment a coach uploads
one, theirs wins. See src/lib/club/placeholder-photos.ts.

Two rules governed the selection:

1. **Free licence only.** All of these are under the
   [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free commercial use, no
   attribution required — recorded anyway so anything shipped is traceable).
   Unsplash+/Getty results are subscription-licensed and were excluded.
2. **Practice shots, never headshots.** The Unsplash License grants no model
   release, so an identifiable person must never appear to be a named coach
   selling sessions. These show the discipline, not a face standing in for
   someone who does not exist.
